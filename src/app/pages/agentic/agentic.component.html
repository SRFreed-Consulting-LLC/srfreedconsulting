Method & adoption

Bringing a team to agentic delivery.

I run a spec-first, human-in-the-loop agentic practice, and I have taken other engineers through the same change — including engineers who did not want to go. Twenty-eight years of instinct made me skeptical that a model could carry real architectural weight. Team-wide adoption of GitLab Duo at the Federal Reserve is what changed my mind, which is exactly why I can argue with a skeptic instead of past one.

What this does not mean

It does not mean the agent decides the architecture, and it does not mean review gets skipped because output arrived quickly. Every number on this page came from a change I specified, read and own. If something on one of these systems breaks at two in the morning, the person answering for it is me — which is precisely why the judgment stays human. A team that adopts the speed without adopting that boundary has bought itself faster defects.
